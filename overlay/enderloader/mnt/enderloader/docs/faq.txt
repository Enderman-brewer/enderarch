Enderloader FAQ
===============

Q: What is Enderloader?
A: A recovery environment for damaged or unbootable Linux systems.
   It scans your disks, lets you mount the root filesystem, and
   provides tools to fix common issues.

Q: How do I chroot into my system?
A: Select option 2 from the menu, or after mounting:
   mount --bind /dev /newroot/dev
   mount --bind /proc /newroot/proc
   mount --bind /sys /newroot/sys
   chroot /newroot /bin/bash

Q: How do I reinstall GRUB?
A: chroot into the system, then:
   grub-install /dev/sdX
   grub-mkconfig -o /boot/grub/grub.cfg

Q: My system won't boot after kernel update, what do I do?
A: Chroot in and downgrade the kernel:
   pacman -U /var/cache/pacman/pkg/linux-<old-version>.pkg.tar.zst

Q: Where are my files?
A: They're in /newroot after mounting. Use:
   ls /newroot/home/
   ls /newroot/etc/
