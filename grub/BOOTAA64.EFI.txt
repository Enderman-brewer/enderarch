BOOTAA64.EFI is built at ISO assembly time using:
grub-mkimage -O arm64-efi -p /boot/grub -o BOOTAA64.EFI -c embed.cfg \
  part_msdos part_gpt fat udf iso9660 squash4 linux loopback search \
  search_fs_file search_label normal configfile echo ls reboot halt \
  efi_gop

'udf' is required: the ISO is an ISO 9660/UDF bridge and UDF is the
filesystem view every modern OS mounts.
